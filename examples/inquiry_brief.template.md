## Domain
The relationship between top management team diversity and firm performance, particularly as mediated by the types of actions a top management team chooses to pursue. 

## Researcher
Brian Fox 

## Current belief summary
Per the documents cited below, there seems to be evidence that top management teams with diversity of background and experience are better able to navigate complex decision environments, which results in their firms having superior performance.  Representative examples are provided in the references section.

## Initiating anomaly
Recent backlash against DEI initiatives and the argument that hiring for diversity results in non-meritocratic hiring, promotion and staffing decisions.  If this is the case, might it be that diversity reduces the average skill level of participants, lowering decision quality and in turn, performance?
